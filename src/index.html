How we build

  1. Measure and draw

    We visit your site or take your measurements, then draw the unit to scale: layout, doors, windows, plumbing and furniture. You sign off the plan and the quote before we cut any steel.

  2. Fabricate in Entebbe

    Frames are cut, welded and painted in our workshop. Panels, glazing, wiring and fittings go in here too, so less work happens on your site.

  3. Deliver and fit

    We transport the unit, set it level on plinths or blocks, connect it and finish decks and trims. Most units are ready to use the same week they arrive.
